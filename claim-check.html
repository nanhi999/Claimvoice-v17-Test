Claim Check — ClaimVoice

A simple Claim Check

1. What happened?

Accident, theft, partial theft, fire, flood, vandalism, total loss/CTL or another event?

2. What is the insurer saying?

Rejected, delayed, partly settled, investigation pending, additional documents or another reason?

3. Is the reason written?

Keep the exact wording and any policy clause identified.

4. What evidence do you have?

Photos, videos, police records where relevant, estimates, invoices, correspondence and timeline.

5. Who has done what?

Was a surveyor appointed? Was an investigation conducted? What remains pending?

6. What do you want next?

Clarification, reconsideration, assessment explanation, payment or formal grievance?

Good next step

Write the issue in one sentence, identify the supporting evidence, and ask one specific question at a time.

Was your stolen vehicle recovered?

If you have already lodged a theft claim and the vehicle has now been recovered, select an answer.

⚠️

Vehicle recovered? Take the security step seriously.

Your vehicle may have a security risk after theft recovery. The thief may have had access to the locks, ignition, keys or other security components.

Get the locks, ignition and relevant security system professionally checked immediately. If a component is compromised, consider replacing it promptly.

Aftermarket replacementKeep the genuine purchase bill/invoice and job card.
Authorised service centreKeep the invoice and job card. Where available, the work may also be verifiable through authorised service history.

This is a practical security precaution. It is not a statement that IRDAI universally mandates lock replacement after every theft recovery.
