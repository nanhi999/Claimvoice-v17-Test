— MOTOR INSURANCE CLAIM HELP IN INDIA —

Understand Your Motor Insurance Claim Before You Accept a Decision

Motor insurance claims can involve accident damage, vehicle theft, fire, flood, partial theft, total loss, constructive total loss (CTL), surveyor assessment, investigation, document requests, deductions and claim rejection. ClaimVoice brings these topics together in simple English so policyholders can understand what normally happens and what questions to ask.

Use ClaimVoice to learn why motor insurance claims get rejected, what documents may be required for an accident or theft claim, how vehicle modifications can affect a claim, what IDV and CTL mean, how to preserve evidence, and what to do when a claim is delayed or disputed.

Every claim is fact-specific. The policy wording, endorsements, documents, circumstances of the loss and applicable requirements should be checked before taking an important decision.
