What is subrogation?

In simple terms, subrogation can allow an insurer, after paying a covered loss, to pursue recovery rights against a responsible third party to the extent permitted by the policy and applicable law. The exact rights and documents depend on the circumstances.

What is an I-Bond or indemnity undertaking?

An insurer may ask for an indemnity undertaking or similar document in particular settlement or recovery situations. The name, wording and legal effect can vary. Do not sign a document you do not understand; ask the insurer to explain what obligation it creates and retain a copy.
