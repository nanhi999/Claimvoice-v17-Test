V11 CHANGE
Added flagship guide: Why Can an Insurer Reject or Dispute a Motor Insurance Claim?
The existing site, branding, navigation and other content are retained.
The new guide covers:
- aftermarket accessories
- vehicle modifications
- electrical fittings/wiring
- engine/ECU changes
- tyres/wheels/suspension/structural changes
- CNG/LPG conversion
- private car used as taxi/commercial use
- hire/reward
- racing/speed testing
- overloading
- licence/permit/compliance
- drunk/drugged driving allegations
- late intimation
- previous damage
- unauthorised repairs
- inconsistent information
- non-disclosure/misrepresentation
- suspicious circumstances
- missing documents
It also explains that a listed circumstance does not automatically make every claim rejectable and gives a practical response checklist.
