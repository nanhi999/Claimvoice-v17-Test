B. SIMPLE EXPLANATION

Subrogation generally concerns recovery rights that may arise after an insurer pays a covered loss, subject to the policy and applicable law. An indemnity undertaking may create a separate promise or protection for the insurer in a particular situation. The exact legal effect depends on the wording and facts.

IMPORTANT: Do not sign a legal undertaking you do not understand. Ask the insurer for its official format and explanation, and obtain professional advice where appropriate.
