Aftermarket Sunroof Modification: Can It Affect Your Car Insurance Claim?

A car leaves the factory without a sunroof. The owner later has an aftermarket sunroof installed by cutting and modifying the roof. Months later, the car suffers an accident, roof damage, leakage or another loss. The insurer asks about the modification. What should the policyholder understand?

ClaimVoice note: A modification, road restriction or documentation issue does not by itself answer every insurance question. The applicable policy wording, endorsements, facts, evidence and law must be considered for the particular claim.

Factory-fitted and aftermarket are different situations

A factory-fitted sunroof forms part of the manufacturer's original vehicle specification. An aftermarket sunroof may involve cutting the roof, adding a frame, drainage channels, electrical wiring and other components. That can make the modification relevant to a later claim.

Why the roof modification can matter

Section 52 of the Motor Vehicles Act regulates alterations to motor vehicles. The precise legal position depends on the alteration, vehicle and applicable rules. For insurance, the insurer may also ask whether the modification was disclosed and whether it has a connection with the claimed damage.

Possible claim situations

  • Water leakage after the installation.
  • Roof damage in a collision.
  • Electrical problems associated with the installation.
  • Damage to the modified roof or surrounding structure.
  • An unrelated accident where the modification is nevertheless noted during inspection.

What should the owner keep?

Keep the installation invoice, installer details, product information, photographs, any technical/approval documents and written communication with the insurer. If the vehicle record or policy needs updating, complete the required process.

If the insurer relies on the modification

Ask for the exact policy clause and the factual/technical basis. If the claimed damage is unrelated, ask the insurer to explain how the modification affects that particular loss rather than accepting a generic statement that the car was modified.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does an aftermarket sunroof automatically cancel insurance?
No. The modification, applicable vehicle rules, policy wording, disclosure and facts of the loss must be considered.
Why can an insurer investigate a sunroof after an accident?
Because the modification may involve structural or electrical work and can be relevant to certain types of damage.
What should I do before installing an aftermarket sunroof?
Check applicable vehicle requirements and ask the insurer whether the modification needs disclosure or endorsement. Keep all installation records.
