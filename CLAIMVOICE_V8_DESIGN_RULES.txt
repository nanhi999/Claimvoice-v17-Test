CLAIMVOICE V8 — VISUAL ORGANISATION RULES

The existing ClaimVoice design is the master. V8 does NOT redesign the visual identity.

HEADER
• Logo on the left.
• Only five primary navigation items: Home, Claim Guides, Policy Decoder, Documents, Real Stories.
• One Share Experience button on the right.
• One menu button on the right.
• All secondary destinations live inside the menu, not scattered across the header.

CONTENT
• Do not add navigation buttons randomly inside paragraphs/cards.
• Cards in the same row must have equal height.
• Actions align at the same baseline.
• Each page has a clear next-step area at the bottom.
• One primary action per section wherever possible.
• Do not duplicate Share Experience.

INFORMATION ARCHITECTURE
• Claim Types contains accident, theft, partial theft, fire, total loss, CTL, flood, vandalism etc.
• Claim Problems contains rejection, delay, partial settlement, deductions, survey/investigation disputes etc.
• Policy Decoder contains technical terms.
• Documents contains checklists/examples.
• Menu contains Forms, Grievance Contacts, Escalation, Modifications and other secondary destinations.
• Search remains the quick route to any topic.

GOAL
A visitor should immediately understand where they are, where they can go next, and what to do—without the interface looking crowded.
