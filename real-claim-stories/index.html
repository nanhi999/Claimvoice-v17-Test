Stories are reviewed before publication

ClaimVoice may edit for clarity, remove personal information and moderate submissions. A submitted story is not automatically proof of every allegation made in it.

How to read a ClaimVoice Real Story

  • What happened: the customer’s description of the incident.
  • Claim problem: rejection, delay, investigation, deduction or another issue.
  • Action taken: what the customer did after the problem arose.
  • Outcome: settlement, rejection, escalation or still pending, where known.
  • Learning: practical points that may help other policyholders.

Real stories are experiences shared by contributors. ClaimVoice does not independently adjudicate disputes and does not decide whether an insurer’s decision is legally or contractually correct.

ClaimVoice is an independent motor-insurance awareness platform. Information is for general awareness and is not legal advice or a determination of whether a particular claim is payable.
