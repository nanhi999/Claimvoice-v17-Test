Tyre & Rim Modifications: Can Modified Wheels Affect a Motor Insurance Claim?

A vehicle owner changes the original wheel and tyre combination for a wider or sportier look. Later the car is involved in an accident. The insurer notices the modified wheels and asks whether the modification was compliant, disclosed or connected with the damage. What should the owner know?

ClaimVoice note: A modification, road restriction or documentation issue does not by itself answer every insurance question. The applicable policy wording, endorsements, facts, evidence and law must be considered for the particular claim.

Not every aftermarket wheel is the same

Changing a cosmetic component is different from changing tyre size, rim diameter, offset, spacers or other components that can affect clearance, steering, suspension or braking. The actual modification matters.

Why the insurer may ask questions

In an accident, the insurer may investigate whether the modified wheels were legal/technically compliant, whether the policy was informed where required, and whether the modification had any relationship with the claimed damage. The Central Motor Vehicles Rules contain requirements concerning tyres and vehicle fitment, so the technical and legal position should be checked rather than assumed.

What to document

  • Tyre make, size and speed/load rating.
  • Rim size and specification.
  • Purchase and installation invoices.
  • Any approval or technical documentation.
  • Photographs before and after installation.
  • Any insurer endorsement or written communication.

If a claim is questioned

Ask whether the insurer is alleging an exclusion, a policy breach, a legal/registration issue or simply that particular damage is unrelated. Ask for the exact clause and the technical evidence supporting the conclusion.

A sensible rule

Before changing the wheel/tyre specification, check the manufacturer's permitted specifications and applicable vehicle rules. If the modification is material, ask the insurer whether disclosure or endorsement is required.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do aftermarket alloy wheels automatically invalidate insurance?
No. The specification, legality, policy terms and relationship to the loss must be considered.
Can oversized tyres cause a claim dispute?
They can create questions about compliance, vehicle dynamics and whether the modification contributed to the loss, depending on the facts.
What if the accident was unrelated to the wheels?
Ask the insurer to explain precisely why the modification affects the particular damage being claimed.
