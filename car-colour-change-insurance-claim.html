ClaimVoice Guide · Vehicle identity & record changes

Changed Your Car's Colour? Can It Affect Your Motor Insurance Claim?

A car is originally registered in one colour. The owner later changes the colour completely but does not update the relevant records or inform the insurer. An accident happens months later. During inspection, the insurer notices that the vehicle colour differs from the records. Can this become a claim issue?

ClaimVoice note: A modification, road restriction or documentation issue does not by itself answer every insurance question. The applicable policy wording, endorsements, facts, evidence and law must be considered for the particular claim.

Why a colour change can matter

Colour is part of the identifying description of a registered vehicle. A major change can therefore create a documentation and vehicle-record issue even though paint itself may not cause an accident.

RC and insurance records should match the vehicle

Section 52 of the Motor Vehicles Act regulates alterations to motor vehicles, and the applicable registration process should be checked for a colour change. Separately, policy changes or material vehicle details should be communicated to the insurer where required.

Does a colour mismatch automatically reject an accident claim?

Do not assume that it does. If the insurer relies on the colour change, ask what exact policy term or legal requirement is being relied upon and how the mismatch affects the particular loss. A documentation issue and the cause of accident damage are not necessarily the same thing.

What should you do after changing colour?

  • Check the applicable RTO process.
  • Update the RC where required.
  • Inform the insurer.
  • Ask whether an endorsement is needed.
  • Keep the paint/work invoice and updated documents.

If the insurer raises the issue during a claim

Request the objection in writing. Ask whether the insurer is alleging an incorrect vehicle description, non-disclosure, policy breach or another ground. Then ask for the exact clause and the evidence supporting the decision.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I change my car's colour without updating the RC?
Check the applicable RTO requirements before making the change; the registration record should accurately reflect the vehicle.
Does a colour change automatically void insurance?
No blanket conclusion should be made. The registration status, disclosure, policy wording and circumstances of the loss matter.
What if the accident had nothing to do with the colour?
Ask the insurer to explain precisely how the colour change affects the particular claim and identify the clause or legal basis being relied upon.
