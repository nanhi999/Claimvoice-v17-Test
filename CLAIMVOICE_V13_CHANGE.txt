ClaimVoice V13
=============
Purpose:
- Preserve the existing V12 visual design and existing content.
- Introduce a scalable content architecture so future articles do not clutter navigation.
- Add ClaimVoice Library and Real Stories hubs.
- Add five new modification/claim-risk guides:
  1. CNG/LPG installation not endorsed in RC or policy
  2. Two-wheeler on prohibited road/highway/expressway
  3. Tyre & rim modifications
  4. Aftermarket sunroof
  5. Vehicle colour change
- Reorganise navigation into Home / Claims / Learn / Real Stories / Documents.
- Keep existing pages accessible through the Explore menu and internal links.
- Reserve Real Stories for future customer stories and animated explainers.
- Update sitemap and search index.

Important:
- New story pages are clearly educational/illustrative unless explicitly identified as documented customer material.
- The articles avoid blanket statements that a modification or violation automatically invalidates every claim.
